Here is how the email system (backend/utils/mailer.js) works in your project right now. It uses a Dual-Channel + Smart Failover Architecture:

1. In-App Portal Channel (Always Instant)
Every time a recruiter sends a Shortlist, Assignment, Interview Invite, Offer Letter, or Joining Letter:

It is saved in MongoDB (Application.emailLogs & Notification collection).
The job seeker immediately sees the alert, meeting link, .ics calendar download, and full letter inside their Notifications & Letters tab (/notifications) and Profile → Applied Jobs table.
2. Real Email Channel (How Actual Gmail Delivery Works)
When the backend runs sendEnterpriseEmail(), it checks your backend/.env file and automatically picks one of two modes:

Mode A: Real Gmail Inbox Delivery (When .env has SMTP credentials)
If you put your SMTP credentials in backend/.env, yes — the email is genuinely delivered straight to the job seeker's real Gmail / Outlook inbox (candidate.email), complete with:

The branded HTML template
The invite.ics calendar attachment (which triggers the "Add to Google Calendar (Yes / Maybe / No)" banner inside Gmail)
The attached HTML Offer / Joining Letter
Reply-To set to the recruiter's email address
Mode B: Automatic Ethereal Sandbox (When .env does NOT have SMTP credentials yet)
Because an email server cannot send through Gmail without an authenticated sender account, if SMTP_HOST / SMTP_USER / SMTP_PASS are not in your backend/.env yet:

Instead of failing, nodemailer automatically creates a temporary Ethereal SMTP Sandbox account on the fly.
It sends the real email + attachments to that sandbox server and returns a previewUrl (https://ethereal.email/message/...).
You see a "View Live Sent Email" button in the UI so you can test and demo the exact email layout without needing credentials first.
How to Enable Real Gmail Inbox Delivery (Takes 60 Seconds, 100% Free)
If you want emails to land in the job seeker's actual Gmail inbox right now:

Go to your Google Account → Security → Enable 2-Step Verification.
Search for "App Passwords" in your Google Account settings (or visit https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords).
Create an App Password named JobPortal — Google will give you a 16-letter code (e.g., abcd efgh ijkl mnop).
Add these 4 lines to your backend/.env file and restart the backend:
SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
SMTP_PORT=587
SMTP_USER=your_gmail_address@gmail.com
SMTP_PASS=abcdefghijklmnop
Once those 4 lines are in backend/.env, getTransporter() in backend/utils/mailer.js automatically switches from "ethereal" to "smtp", and every interview invite, assignment, offer letter, and joining letter will arrive in the candidate's real Gmail inbox within seconds.
